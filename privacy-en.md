# Yamal Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 5 November 2026. This policy takes effect on the date it is published on this page.

## 1. Who we are
Yamal is an iPhone app that helps you organise your salary and spending. It is developed and published by an individual developer, Rashid Alfahad (the "developer", "we"). The developer is responsible for the app and for this policy, and is shown as the app's seller on the App Store.
The app is not affiliated with any bank and does not connect to your bank accounts.

## 2. In short
**We do not collect any data about you.** Everything you enter in Yamal stays on your device. The app sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else. It requires no account or sign-in, requests no iOS permissions, and uses no analytics, tracking or advertising tools.

## 3. Data the app processes on your device
The app processes, on your device only, what you enter or what a shortcut you created passes to it:
- your name or chosen nickname, and your payday;
- your salary and its split into "sahharat" (envelopes);
- expenses: amount, type, category, merchant name and date. For expenses logged from bank messages, it also stores the bank name and the last four digits of the card, if the message contains them;
- **quick-entry text:** if you log an expense through the «أضف مصروف» ("Add expense") action in Shortcuts or Siri, the app extracts the amount and merchant from the description you typed and saves that description as a note on the expense, on your device. The app may save the description in the same way when you use quick entry inside the app. Do not type anything you do not want stored;
- goals and loans you enter, including any profit (interest) rate you set.

Some of this may be personal financial information, but it never leaves your device.

## 4. Shortcuts, Siri and bank messages
You can create an automation in the Shortcuts app that runs when a message arrives from your bank and passes its text to Yamal's «سجّل رسالة البنك» ("Log bank message") action.
- The app extracts the details listed in section 3 on your device. **It does not store the full bank message text.**
- The app ignores one-time passcode (OTP) messages.
- Yamal does not read your messages inbox, because iOS does not allow apps to do so. It only receives the text that your own shortcut passes to it.
- The app also offers two other actions: «أضف مصروف» ("Add expense", see section 3) and «كم باقي لي اليوم؟» ("How much do I have left today?"). The second shows your remaining amount in the Siri or Shortcuts response, according to your device settings.
- You control the automation and can edit, pause or delete it at any time. The Shortcuts app and Siri are governed by Apple's policies.

## 5. Storage and the widget
Your data is stored on your device in a local storage container shared by the app and its widget (an App Group). The widget shows a summary that the app saves in this container. This sharing happens only within your device, and no data leaves the device as a result.

## 6. No sharing, selling or tracking
We do not sell your data or share it with any third party, and we do not use it for advertising. We do not track you within the app or across other apps and websites.

## 7. Backups
If you back up your device through iCloud or your computer, Yamal's data may be included in those backups according to your device and Apple account settings. These backups are governed by Apple's policies, and the developer has no access to them.

## 8. Retention and deletion
Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Two ways to delete all of it are always available:
- **Erase all data:** on the home screen, tap the Yamal button (gear icon), then Settings («الإعدادات»), then «مسح كل البيانات» ("Erase all data"). This deletes all app data, including the widget summary.
- **Removing the app:** deleting the app from your device deletes its local data.

You can also delete some items individually: goals, loans, and an expense awaiting review, by marking it as incorrect. Whether other items can be edited or deleted individually, including expenses after you confirm them, depends on the version of the app you are using. Anything your version does not let you delete individually can be removed using either of the two ways above.

Backups you created earlier in iCloud or on your computer may remain until you delete them in your device or account settings. "Erase all data" does not delete automations you created in the Shortcuts app, so delete those in Shortcuts.

## 9. Your rights and the app's limitations
You can view your data within the app and delete it as described in section 8. The app does not currently let you export or import your data.
Because we do not collect your data, the developer holds no copy of it to access, correct or delete on your behalf. The app does not ask for your consent to data collection, because it collects no data. To stop bank messages from being passed to the app, delete the automation in the Shortcuts app.

## 10. Children
The app is not directed at children, and it does not collect data from any user, including children.

## 11. Security
The protection of your data depends on the security of your device itself, so keep it locked with a passcode and keep iOS up to date. No storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 12. Applicable law
The developer operates from the State of Kuwait and complies with its regulations as they apply to the app. If we ever add a feature that requires collecting data, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before any collection.

## 13. Changes
We may update this policy. We will post the updated version on this page with a new "last updated" date.

## 14. Contact
Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer at: [yamal.app.support@gmail.com](mailto:yamal.app.support@gmail.com)
