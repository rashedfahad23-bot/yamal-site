# Yamal Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 5 November 2026. This policy takes effect on the date it is published on this page.

## 1. Who we are
Yamal is an iPhone app that helps you organise your salary and spending. It is developed and published by an individual developer, Rashid Alfahad (the "developer", "we"). The developer is responsible for the app and for this policy, and is shown as the app's seller on the App Store.
The app is not affiliated with any bank and does not connect to your bank accounts.

## 2. In short
**We do not collect any data about you.** Everything you enter in Yamal stays on your device. The app sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else. It requires no account or sign-in, uses no AI model and no server, requests no iOS permissions (including microphone access), and uses no analytics, tracking or advertising tools.

## 3. Data the app processes on your device
The app processes, on your device only, what you enter or what a shortcut you created passes to it:
- your name or the nickname you choose to be addressed by, and your payday;
- **your money manager:** the name you choose for the in-app money manager and the persona you pick for it. The persona changes only the tone of replies, not the numbers;
- **getting-to-know-you questions:** your answers to the optional getting-to-know-you questions (20 questions);
- **money basics** you enter, such as salary, obligations, debts, savings and emergency fund;
- your salary and its split into "sahharat" (envelopes);
- **weekly spending:** the items you set and their percentages, and the catch-up method you choose;
- recorded expenses and salaries: amount, type, category, merchant name and date;
- **what is saved from bank messages:** for each expense logged from a bank message, the app saves the amount, the merchant name, the bank name, the last four digits of the card if the message contains them, and the date of the message;
- **quick-entry text:** if you log an expense through the «أضف مصروف» ("Add expense") action in Shortcuts or Siri, the app extracts the amount and merchant from the description you typed and saves that description as a note on the expense. When you use quick entry inside the app, it may save the description in the same way. So do not type anything you do not want stored on your device;
- **chat:** what you type in the chat, and what you confirm saving from it (see section 5);
- goals (shown in the app as «لؤلؤة», "pearl"), including the rewards fund, and loans you enter, including any profit (interest) rate you set. Nothing is moved into the rewards fund without your agreement;
- merchant categorisation rules, and settings, including language, digits and appearance settings.

Some of this may be personal financial information, but it never leaves your device.

## 4. Shortcuts, Siri and bank messages
You can create an automation in the Shortcuts app that runs when a message arrives from your bank and passes its text to Yamal's «سجّل رسالة البنك» ("Log bank message") action.
- The app extracts the details listed in section 3 on your device. **It does not store the full bank message text.**
- The app ignores one-time passcode (OTP) messages.
- Yamal does not read your messages inbox, because iOS does not allow apps to do so. It only receives the text that your own shortcut passes to it.
- The app also offers two other actions: «أضف مصروف» ("Add expense", see section 3) and «كم باقي لي اليوم؟» ("How much do I have left today?"). The second shows your remaining amount in the Siri or Shortcuts response, according to your device settings.
- You control the automation and can edit, pause or delete it at any time. The Shortcuts app and Siri are governed by Apple's policies.

## 5. Chat, reports and dictation
- **How replies and reports are worked out:** replies in the «أقدر أشتريه؟» ("Can I afford it?") chat, and the weekly and monthly reports, are calculated on your device using fixed calculation rules, with no AI model and no server. Nothing you type in the chat is sent off your device.
- **Logging an expense from the chat:** the app shows a draft and saves it only after you confirm.
- **Dictation:** you can enter text using the iOS keyboard's dictation feature. The app itself does not record or receive audio and does not request microphone access. It only receives the text that the keyboard enters, and sends none of it off your device. Dictation is an iOS feature governed by your device settings and Apple's privacy policy.

## 6. Storage and on-device tools
Your data is stored on your device only, in a local storage container shared by the app and its on-device tools (an App Group). This sharing happens only within your device.
- **Widgets,** including the pearl widget (PearlWidget): these show a summary of your data, such as this week's summary, wherever you add them.
- **Payday Live Activity:** this appears on the Lock Screen, and in the Dynamic Island on devices that have it.
- **Quick-add control in Control Center** (iOS 18 or later): this opens the app for quick entry through its internal link `yamal://add`.

What widgets and the payday Live Activity show may be visible to anyone who can see your phone's screen, including the Lock Screen. If you do not want this, remove the widgets or turn off Live Activities for Yamal in iOS Settings.

The app does not use iCloud sync or the Keychain, does not send you notifications, does not export your data to files, and does not send any data off your device.

## 7. No sharing, selling or tracking
We do not sell your data or share it with any third party, and we do not use it for advertising. We do not track you within the app or across other apps and websites.

## 8. Backups
If you back up your device through iCloud or your computer, those backups may include Yamal's data. These backups are under your control and are governed by Apple's settings and policies; the developer has no access to them. Neither "Erase all data" nor deleting the app changes a backup taken before it, and you can delete such backups in your device or Apple account settings. If you restore an older backup, the data it contains may be restored with it.

## 9. Editing, deletion and retention
**Retention:** your data stays on your device until you erase all of it with the "Erase all data" button, delete an individual item that can be deleted, or delete the app, as described below. The developer keeps no copy, because the data never reaches the developer.

**Erasing all data:** on the home screen, tap the "Settings" button (labelled «يامال ⚙︎» in the Arabic interface), then "Erase all data" («مسح كل البيانات» in the Arabic interface). The app asks you to confirm ("Erase all data?") before erasing, and the erasure cannot be undone once confirmed. It deletes from your device:
- recorded transactions (expenses and salaries);
- the salary split and sahharat;
- your money manager's name and persona, your getting-to-know-you answers and your money basics;
- weekly spending items and the catch-up method;
- goals («لؤلؤة»), including the rewards fund;
- loans;
- merchant categorisation rules;
- settings, including language, digits and appearance settings.

After erasing, the app returns to its start screen, the payday Live Activity ends, and the on-device tools show empty values.

**Editing:** you can edit:
- the salary amount, the sahharat split, your payday and how the app addresses you;
- in Settings: your money manager's name and persona, your getting-to-know-you answers, and the catch-up method;
- in the «العرض» (Display) section of Settings: language, digits and appearance;
- on the «مصروف الأسبوع» (weekly spending) screen: your weekly spending items.

**Deleting an individual item:** you can delete a single goal («لؤلؤة») or loan, and you can delete a bank message awaiting review by marking it as incorrect.

**Recorded transactions:** recorded transactions (expenses and salaries) are currently removed with "Erase all data".

**Deleting the app:** iOS is expected to delete the app's data stored on the device when the app is deleted. Backups taken before then are covered by section 8.

**Shortcuts:** "Erase all data" does not delete automations you created in the Shortcuts app, so delete them there if you wish.

## 10. Your rights and the app's limitations
You can view your data within the app, edit what can be edited, and delete it as described in section 9. The app does not currently let you export or import your data.
Because we do not collect your data, the developer holds no copy of it to access, correct or delete on your behalf. The app does not ask for your consent to data collection, because it collects no data. To stop bank messages from being passed to the app, delete the automation in the Shortcuts app.

## 11. Children
The app is not directed at children, and it does not collect data from any user, including children.

## 12. Security
The protection of your data depends on the security of your device itself, so keep it locked with a passcode and keep iOS up to date. No storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 13. Applicable law
The developer operates from the State of Kuwait and complies with its regulations as they apply to the app. If we ever add a feature that requires collecting data, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before any collection.

## 14. Changes
We may update this policy. We will post the updated version on this page with a new "last updated" date.

## 15. Contact
