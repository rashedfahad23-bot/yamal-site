# Yamal Support

Yamal is an iPhone app, in Arabic and English, for organising your salary and spending. It splits your salary into "chests", tracks your weekly spending, expenses, goals and loans, and includes a chat with a money manager whose name and persona you choose. All your data stays on your device. The app is developed and published by an individual developer.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is my data private?
Yes. The app sends no data to the developer or to anyone else, and it needs no account or sign-in. For details, see the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy-en/' | relative_url }}).

### Does the «أقدر أشتريه؟» ("Can I afford it?") chat send my data anywhere?
No. Chat replies and the weekly and monthly reports are calculated on your device using fixed calculation rules, with no AI model and no server. If you ask the chat to log an expense, the app shows a draft and saves it only after you confirm.

### Does the app listen to my voice?
No. The app does not record audio and does not request microphone access. You can enter text using the iOS keyboard's dictation, and the app receives only the text. Dictation is an iOS feature governed by your device settings and Apple's policy.

### Is there a tour of the app?
Yes. After the getting-to-know-you questions, a short tour, "How Yamal works" («طريقة يامال» in the Arabic interface), shows each part of the app with a real screen. "Skip" closes it; "Settings" › "How Yamal works" opens it again.

### How do I change my money manager's name or persona?
In the app's Settings. The persona changes only the tone of replies, not the numbers. You can also edit your getting-to-know-you answers and the catch-up method in Settings.

### How do I change the language, digits or appearance?
In Settings, under the «العرض» (Display) section.

### What do the Home Screen and Lock Screen tools show?
- **Widgets,** including the pearl widget: these show a summary of your data.
- **Payday Live Activity:** this appears on the Lock Screen. It does not show your salary amount: it shows "Your salary is ready for Salary day · N days to payday".

Anyone who can see your phone's screen may see this. If you do not want this, remove the widgets or turn off Live Activities for Yamal in iOS Settings. The quick-add control in Control Center opens the app for quick entry.

### Which devices does the app support?
Yamal is designed for iPhone only. There is no dedicated iPad version.

### How do I log expenses automatically from bank messages?
Follow the seven steps in "Settings" › "Log bank messages automatically" («تسجيل رسائل البنك تلقائي» in the Arabic interface). Each step has a small drawing. In short, you create an automation in the Shortcuts app that runs when a message arrives from your bank, add Yamal's "Log Bank Message" («تسجيل رسالة البنك») action to it, and pass the message content to that action.
- **Sender:** type the bank's name exactly as Messages shows it, for example BoubyanBank, as one word.
- **Message:** in Yamal's action, pick "Shortcut Input", then "Content". Do not type these words.
- **iOS versions:** the Shortcuts app looks a little different on each iOS version. Look for "Automation", "Message", "Run Immediately" and Yamal's "Log Bank Message" action.

The app does not store the full bank message text. With the expense, it saves the amount, the merchant name, the bank name, the last four digits of the card if the message contains them, and the date of the message. It ignores one-time passcode (OTP) messages.
Shortcuts action names and Siri phrases follow your iPhone's language, not the language chosen in the app. On an iPhone set to English, Yamal's actions appear under "Yamal" as "Log Bank Message", "Log Spending" and "What's Left Today?"; on an iPhone set to Arabic, they appear under «يامال» as «تسجيل رسالة البنك», «تسجيل مصروف» and «كم باقي لي اليوم؟».

### What is saved when I use quick entry?
If you log an expense through the "Log Spending" («تسجيل مصروف») action in Shortcuts or Siri, the app extracts the amount and merchant from the description you typed and saves that description as a note on the expense. When you use quick entry inside the app, it may save the description in the same way. All of this stays on your device only.

### What can I edit?
- The salary amount, the split into chests, your payday and how the app addresses you.
- In Settings: your money manager's name and persona, your getting-to-know-you answers, and the catch-up method.
- In the «العرض» (Display) section of Settings: language, digits and appearance.
- On the «مصروف الأسبوع» (weekly spending) screen: your weekly spending items.
- In "Anchor" («المرساة»): a loan's name, lender, balance, profit rate, installment and due day. Tap the loan to edit it.
- In "Pearls" («اللؤلؤ»): a goal's name, target, monthly amount and date. Tap the goal to edit it; what it has saved is kept.
- In "All" («الكل»): a transaction's category. Swipe the transaction to change it, or swipe it the other way for "Not me" («مو أنا») to leave it out of every total.

Changing the payday in Settings asks you to confirm first. The current voyage then ends on the new payday, and your plan stays as it is.

### How do I delete my data?
- **All data:** on the home screen, tap the "Settings" button (labelled «يامال ⚙︎» in the Arabic interface), then "Erase all data" («مسح كل البيانات» in the Arabic interface), and confirm when asked "Erase all data?". The erasure cannot be undone once confirmed. It deletes from your device:
  - recorded transactions (expenses and salaries);
  - the salary split and chests;
  - your money manager's name and persona, your getting-to-know-you answers and your money basics;
  - weekly spending items and the catch-up method;
  - goals («لؤلؤة»), including the rewards fund;
  - loans;
  - merchant categorisation rules;
  - settings, including language, digits and appearance.

  The app then returns to its start screen, the payday Live Activity ends, and the on-device tools show empty values.
- **A single item:** you can delete a single goal («لؤلؤة») or loan, and you can delete a bank message awaiting review by marking it as incorrect.
- **A single transaction:** in "All" («الكل»), press and hold it, then "Delete transaction" («حذف العملية»), and confirm. This cannot be undone.
- **Deleting the app:** iOS is expected to delete the app's data stored on the device when the app is deleted.

Neither erasing nor deleting the app changes a backup of your device taken earlier through iCloud or a computer. For details, see sections 8 and 9 of the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy-en/' | relative_url }}).

### The app misread a bank message. What can I do?
If the message is awaiting review, you can mark it as incorrect to delete it. Once a transaction is recorded, you can delete it: press and hold it in "All" («الكل»), then "Delete transaction" («حذف العملية»), and confirm.

### Can I move my data to a new device?
The app does not currently offer data export or import. Your data may carry over if you restore a backup of your old device onto the new one, depending on your backup settings.

### Is the app free?
Yes, the current version is free.

### Is the app linked to any bank?
No. Yamal is not affiliated with any bank, does not connect to your bank accounts, and does not carry out any financial transactions.

## Financial disclaimer
Yamal is a tool for organising and recording your salary and spending. It is not a bank, lender or investment firm, and it does not provide financial, investment, tax or legal advice. The figures and suggestions it shows, including loan, profit and goal calculations and repayment order, are estimates based on what you enter and may differ from your actual accounts or a lender's terms. Before making any significant financial decision, please consult a licensed or qualified professional.
